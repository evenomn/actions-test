### 【严重】D-Link DIR-600L栈溢出远程利用
[CVE-2026-133993](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-133993) · CVSS 10.0 · P1 重点关注 · 披露 2026-11-21
类型: 内存破坏/栈溢出 | 利用条件: 远程·免认证,难度低
摘要: 远程攻击者可向gena.cgi的service参数传入超长数据，触发栈溢出，可能导致代码执行。
复现价值: 强烈推荐 — 访问gena.cgi的GENA订阅处理，向service参数传超长数据触发溢出;影响面 边界路由器，公网管理面暴露
处置建议: 联系厂商升级固件；临时限制gena.cgi访问
原文: A security flaw has been discovered in D-Link DIR-600L 2.17b02. The exploit has been released to the public and may be used for attacks.
资料: [NVD 详情](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-133993) · [参考](https://github.com/ruanyi101/router-firmware-security-advisories/blob/main/router-firmware-GitHub-advisories/related-findings/D-Link-DIR-600L-gena-cgi-stack-overflow-review.md) · [参考](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-133993)
